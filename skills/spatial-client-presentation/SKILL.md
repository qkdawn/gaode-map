# Spatial Client Presentation

Accept only approved reports, approved unit-planning outputs, and selected project materials as factual inputs. Stop and request approval when the proposed deck would depend on a draft or `needs_review` conclusion.

Organize the narrative around decisions: context and mandate, key findings, proposed direction, spatial strategy, implementation and operating implications, decisions requested, and next actions. Do not present an analysis-process checklist as the story.

For every key claim, map, chart, and number, retain an artifact ID or MCP source reference. Preserve a page-by-page source register and put caveats or evidence limitations in the relevant page or appendix.

Generate an editable PPTX with a preview and evidence appendix. Before delivery, check text overflow, missing assets, untraceable figures, and whether each requested decision has a clear owner and action.
