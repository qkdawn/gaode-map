# Stage 1 Report Template

Write a professional Chinese report titled:

`《[项目名称]区域研判、项目定位与设计前置策划报告》`

## 1. 执行摘要

State the preferred direction, target users, problem solved, decisive evidence, prerequisites, fallback option, and three priority actions.

## 2. 任务与研究边界

State decision questions, project boundary, wider analysis scopes, dates, methods, and what the report cannot determine.

## 3. 资料系统与可信度

List source groups, readiness, project facts versus design intent, major conflicts, missing inputs, and evidence limitations.

## 4. 区域结构研判

Explain regional role, access, land-use and service relationships, ecological/spatial structure, nodes, corridors, barriers, and interfaces.

## 5. 人群、活动与需求线索

Describe evidenced user groups and demand proxies. Separate current observations from hypotheses requiring survey.

## 6. 地方文化与文旅资产

Identify locally grounded assets, audience journeys, content opportunities, and generic-tourism risks.

## 7. 存量空间与更新条件

Explain adaptable assets, retain/adapt/replace principles, implementation constraints, governance, and staged activation.

## 8. 机会主题与备选定位

Present 2–4 distinct options. For every option show users, program, spatial carrier, operation, evidence, prerequisites, risks, and validation.

## 9. 决策矩阵与推荐定位

Compare options qualitatively. Give a conditional recommendation and reasons for rejecting or deferring alternatives.

## 10. 功能组合

Define:

- must-have core programs;
- supporting programs;
- public/community programs;
- event or temporary programs;
- back-of-house requirements;
- excluded or cautionary programs;
- ranges or proportions that require design verification.

## 11. 空间策略

Define zones, anchors, connections, public interfaces, arrival, movement, day/night use, shared space, and links to surrounding districts or assets.

## 12. 内容、运营与治理

Define daily content, event rhythm, operators, tenants, partnerships, community participation, management, and operating assumptions.

## 13. 分期与验证计划

Separate immediate evidence collection, low-cost pilots, medium-term adaptation, and long-term construction. Attach a decision gate to each phase.

## 14. 设计任务书

Translate strategy into requirements and open design questions. Do not pre-empt architectural solutions.

## 15. 风险、缺口与下一步

Disclose unresolved conflicts, survey/approval requirements, invalidation conditions, and next actions.

## Appendix A. Claim—Evidence Table

List claim ID, claim type, text, evidence refs, locator summary, limitations, and report section.

## Appendix B. Sources and Methods

List source metadata, data years, analysis methods, spatial scopes, and known limitations.

## Writing rules

- Lead each section with a judgment, then evidence, implication, and limitation.
- Use local named places only when present in read evidence.
- Label hypotheses and recommendations explicitly.
- Avoid generic slogans, repetitive role summaries, and unsupported precision.
- Prefer a smaller number of decisive maps/tables over exhaustive metric dumps.
